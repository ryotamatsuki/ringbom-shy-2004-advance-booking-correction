# Correction / Reassessment Workflow

This repository uses a gated research workflow.

## Stage 0 — Evidence Freeze
Freeze identity, sources, master-audit provenance, the exact transferred discrepancy, prior-disclosure search, and a reproducible baseline.

## Stage 1 — Model Canonicalization
Rewrite players, timing, strategy spaces, information, feasible sets, tie rules, payoffs, and maintained parameter restrictions.

## Stage 2 — Independent Clean-Room Derivation
Re-derive every claim needed for the proposed correction without importing algebra from the master-audit conclusion.

## Stage 3 — Boundary and Global-Deviation Audit
Check clipping points, equality cases, regime switches, entry/exit, market capture, and all relevant feasible deviations.

## Stage 4 — Corrected Result Freeze
State corrected propositions/theorems with exact domains. Separate certified results from conjectures and unresolved branches.

## Stage 5 — Downstream Impact
Recompute comparative statics, profits, consumer surplus, welfare, calibration, figures, and policy statements that depend on the corrected object.

## Stage 6 — Independent Adversarial Recheck
Run a fresh proof audit, symbolic/numerical regression tests, and formal verification where appropriate.

## Stage 7 — Manuscript Construction
Draft the shortest paper that fully proves the contribution. Keep correction, extension, and unchanged original results separate.

## Stage 8 — Novelty / Journal Fit / Submission QA
Repeat the prior-disclosure search against the final theorem set, select the journal ladder, freeze reproducibility assets, and run submission QA.

## Rule
A later stage may not silently repair an earlier failed gate. Re-open the earliest affected stage and record the change.
