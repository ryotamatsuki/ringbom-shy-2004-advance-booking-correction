## Appendix, supplement, and predecessor

- No appendix or online supplement is listed in the publisher record or present in the eight-page VOR.
- No author code or replication data are listed.
- The VOR cites Ringbom and Shy (2003), “Reservations and Refunds,” and describes it as analyzing fully refundable reservation systems without partial refunds. A Hanken/Finna catalog identifies a 2002 working paper by the same authors, “Advance Booking, Reservations and Refunds,” in the Yrjö Jahnsson Working Paper Series in Industrial Economics, no. 10. The exact title/version lineage is not resolved because the 2003 item’s full text was not recovered in this audit. Neither bibliographic record is silently treated as the same document.
- The distinct 2003 working paper “Reservations, Refunds, and Price Competition” is listed separately in the Hanken series catalogue. An author-hosted 2004 paper, “Advance Booking and Refunds Under Capacity Constraints,” was also inspected; its capacity-constrained mechanism differs from the VOR's exogenous-price, unlimited-capacity two-type refund comparison.
