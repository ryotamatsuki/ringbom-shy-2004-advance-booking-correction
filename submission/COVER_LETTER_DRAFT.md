Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“A Correction to the Two-Type Refund Result in Ringbom and Shy (2004)”** as a **Comment** for *Economics Bulletin*.

The note revisits the two-type advance-booking model in Staffan Ringbom and Oz Shy (2004), “Advance Booking, Cancellations, and Partial Refunds,” *Economics Bulletin* 13(1), 1–7. The condition printed in their equation (6) and Proposition 1 omits a factor (1/(1-sigma_L)) from the high-type profit loss. The omission is economically consequential: an exact parameter point inside the model’s stated domain makes the printed condition select the lower-profit refund.

The manuscript derives the corrected endpoint comparison directly from primitive payoffs, characterizes the complete refund-choice correspondence on (r\in[0,1]) under the source’s weak-participation convention, and explains the resulting qualification to the first corollary. It also independently rechecks the later uniform-type section. Those welfare results survive on their active domain, while two numerical private-refund entries in Table 1 do not reproduce from the paper’s own formula.

The contribution is intentionally narrow. The manuscript does not claim a new general screening theorem or that the article as a whole is invalid. Its purpose is to provide a reproducible correction of a published result and to distinguish precisely what changes from what survives. Exact-rational verification code, independent adversarial checks, and proof-assistant artifacts are maintained in the companion repository.
