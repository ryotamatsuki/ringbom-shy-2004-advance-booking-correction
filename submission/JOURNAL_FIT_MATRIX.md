## Ladder

- **Stretch:** Review of Industrial Organization.
- **Primary:** Economics Bulletin, submitted specifically as a **Comment** if the authenticated portal preserves the current public article type.
- **Realistic fallback:** Economics and Business Letters, after confirming in its live portal that a third-party mathematical correction is admissible as an original theoretical letter.
- **Safety:** **No separate evidence-backed safety venue is designated.** A lower-fit journal is not called “safety” merely to fill a ladder slot. If both the source-journal Comment route and the short-letter fallback reject the contribution on scope/significance, the workflow should reassess publication value rather than journal-shop indiscriminately.

## Why the primary is not the stretch

The frozen paper is five pages because the correction is mathematically self-contained and the surrounding uniform-model results mostly survive. *Economics Bulletin* currently supplies the most specific positive evidence: it is the source journal and explicitly accepts peer-reviewed Comments under a seven-page cap. *Review of Industrial Organization* is field-relevant and explicitly welcomes shorter notes/commentaries, but its broader statement emphasizes significant IO theory. The current evidence therefore supports using RIO as an optional stretch rather than forcing the paper there first.
